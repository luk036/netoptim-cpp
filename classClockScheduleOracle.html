Detailed Description

template<typename Graph, typename Mapping>
class ClockScheduleOracle< Graph, Mapping >

Oracle for minimum-period / maximum-yield clock skew scheduling.

Node potentials are the clock arrival times; the iterate is \(x = (\mathrm{TCP}, \beta)\) and the objective is \(\min \mathrm{TCP} / \beta\) subject to the timing constraint graph being feasible. The graph's edges are netoptim_detail::timing_edge; a negative cycle, if any, supplies the separating cutting plane.

A negative cycle at \(x\) gives the cut \((g, f)\) with \(f = -\sum_{e \in C} w(e)\) and \(g = (-\#\{\text{setup edges}\}, |C|)\).
